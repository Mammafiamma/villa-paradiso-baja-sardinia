# 📖 The Villa Paradiso "Local API" & Survival Guide

Welcome to the official documentation for your stay at **Villa Paradiso**. Here you will find all the necessary "endpoints" to maximize your productivity and relaxation in Costa Smeralda.

## ☕ 1. Fuel & Productivity (Food & Groceries)
To keep your system running, you'll need high-quality inputs.
* **Supermarket (Local Cache):** *Dettori Market* - Just a 3-minute drive. Perfect for daily fresh local products.
* **Gourmet BBQ Setup:** The outdoor induction kitchen and BBQ are ready for deployment. We recommend buying fresh fish at the local market in San Pantaleo (Thursday mornings).
* **Coffee Protocol:** The kitchen is fully equipped with an espresso machine to compile your morning code.

## 🏖 2. System Reboot (Beaches & Wellness)
When you need to cool down your CPU, execute these commands:
* **Cala Battistoni (Baja Sardinia):** 200m away. Your default beach.
* **Spiaggia del Principe:** 15 min drive. One of the most beautiful white-sand beaches in the world.
* **The Villa Jacuzzi:** Heated and ready 24/7. Best used during sunset with a glass of Vermentino wine.

## 🍸 3. Nightlife & Networking (Social Endpoints)
Costa Smeralda is famous for its high-end networking events (aka parties).
* **Phi Beach:** The ultimate sunset club. Built into the rocks, it's the best place in Sardinia for a sunset drink.
* **Ritual Club:** A club carved entirely out of a granite castle. A must-see architectural masterpiece.
* **Promenade du Port (Porto Cervo):** 10 min drive. High-end shopping, art galleries, and luxury yachts. 

## 🔌 4. Hardware Details
* **Power Outlets:** Standard Italian/European plugs (Type C/F/L). *Tip: Bring universal adapters if coming from the US/UK.*
* **Climate Control:** Air conditioning in all master rooms to keep the servers (you) from overheating.
* **Smart TV:** Connected to the Starlink network. Ready for Netflix, Prime, or casting your presentations.

> **Need Support?** If you encounter any "bugs" during your stay, your host is available via WhatsApp for immediate troubleshooting.
